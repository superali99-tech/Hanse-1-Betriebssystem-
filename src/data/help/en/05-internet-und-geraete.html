Internet, Wi-Fi and devices

Wi-Fi and Ethernet

Click the network icon in the system tray, choose a network and enter the password. Wired connections are set up automatically.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth icon → Add device. Headphones, mice and keyboards are paired and reconnected automatically.

Sound and microphone

Volume icon: volume, output device and microphone. NovaOS uses PipeWire.

Printers

Most network and USB printers are detected automatically (CUPS, IPP Everywhere). Manually: Nova Settings → Printers.

Displays

Connect a second monitor and arrange it in Nova Settings → Display and Monitor. Meta+P switches modes.

USB drives

Drives appear in the tray and in Nova Files under Devices. Use Safely remove before unplugging.

Power

Battery icon: charge level and power profile. Sleep, restart and shut down are in the start menu.
