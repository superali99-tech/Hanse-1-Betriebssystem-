Users and privacy

User accounts

Manage users, passwords and administrator rights in Nova Settings → Users. Passwords are only stored as secure hashes in /etc/shadow.

Screen lock

Meta+L locks the screen; it locks automatically after 10 minutes.

Guests

Create a standard account without administrator rights for guests (Nova Settings → Users → Add New User).

Privacy

NovaOS contains no telemetry. Network connections only happen through your usage or updates (Debian, Flathub). Nova Store fetches ratings from odrs.gnome.org (can be disabled).
