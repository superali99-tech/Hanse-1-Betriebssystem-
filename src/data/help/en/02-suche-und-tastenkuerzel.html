Search and shortcuts

Central search

Press Alt+Space or just start typing on the empty desktop. Search finds apps, files and settings, calculates (12*7) and converts units (10 km in mi).

Important shortcuts

  • Meta – start menu
  • Alt+Space – central search
  • Meta+W – overview of windows and virtual desktops
  • Meta+Ctrl+←/→ – switch virtual desktop
  • Meta+←/→ – snap window to left/right half
  • Meta+PgUp – maximize
  • Meta+L – lock screen
  • Ctrl+Esc – task manager
  • Print – screenshot
  • Meta+V – clipboard history
